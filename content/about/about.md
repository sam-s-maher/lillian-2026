description: |
  Australian vocalist, composer and multi-instrumentalist Lillian Albazi has emerged as one of Australia’s most versatile and compelling contemporary artists, forging a career that moves fearlessly between jazz, experimental music,  alternative and noise.

  Albazi first gained national attention after being invited to perform at the 2016 Melbourne International Jazz Festival while still a student under the artistic curation of Michael Tortoni and Melanie Pose. This was her first live show and the beginning of her jazz career. Since then, she has established herself as a distinctive voice within Australia’s contemporary music landscape, headlining at venues and festivals including the Melbourne Recital Centre, MONA, Devonport Jazz Festival, Jazz Music Institute, Brisbane International Jazz Festival, JazzLab, The Citadel, Boroondara Arts and leading jazz clubs/venues throughout Australia alongside mentors, colleagues and friends. 

  Albazi has become recognised for her deeply expressive vocal style, modern interpretations of the Great American Songbook and original compositions that bridge traditional jazz language with contemporary improvisation and art music influences. Her acclaimed 2021 debut album “After-Image” further cemented her reputation as an artist capable of balancing technical sophistication with expression and depth coupled with smart arranging. 

  Beyond jazz, Albazi’s work consistently pushes genre boundaries. She fronts Melbourne alternative outfit Great \~ Falls, a project blending indie, noise textures and experimental songwriting, earning praise from Australian radio and independent press for it’s intensity and sonic unpredictability. Simultaneously, she expanded her international profile through collaborations with the US-based noise rock group Great Falls, touring to the UK and contributing to the critically celebrated release “Objects Without Pain”, which received widespread acclaim across the world, including recognition from publications such as Stereogum and Brooklyn Vegan. 

  Albazi’s experimental practice further extends into avant-garde and chamber-inspired performance through projects such as Bright Phantoms, her collaborative work with bassist Oscar Neyland. Exploring extended vocal techniques, altered tunings and modern classical influences, the project highlights Albazi’s ongoing fascination with improvisation, texture and the intersection of jazz and contemporary art music. 

  Although it seems she has only just begun, Lillian Albazi’s music career has taken her across Australia, New Zealand, the United Kingdom and Europe, performing sold-out shows and collaborating with internationally respected artists, labels, distributors, composers and audio engineers across multiple genres and many countries. Whether appearing in intimate jazz settings, large concert halls, experimental ensembles or high-volume alternative music environments, Albazi is recognised for her fearless artistry, commanding stage presence and unique ability to move authentically between musical worlds. 

  Widely regarded as one of Australia’s most dynamic emerging musical voices, Lillian Albazi continues to redefine genre boundaries while building an internationally respected body of work grounded in honesty, experimentation and uncompromising musicality. Lillian loves her first love, jazz and coming home to her fourteen year old cat Maggie with snacks for her to sample from the places that she’s played.
